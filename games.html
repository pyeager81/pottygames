Snap & Swing

A single-tap 2D physics arcade game. Your orb continuously rises — tap to fire a tether at the nearest anchor point, hold to swing around it, and release to launch yourself toward the next one. Dodge hazards, chain stars together, and see how far one run can take you.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to tether to the nearest anchor
  • Hold to keep swinging and build momentum
  • Release at the right moment to launch and dodge barriers
  • Chain stars together for combo score

The one-tap stacking game made for quick breaks. A roll of toilet paper slides back and forth — tap to drop it on the stack. Whatever hangs over the edge gets trimmed off, so every miss makes the next drop harder. Land it perfectly and you get a burst of soap bubbles; three perfects in a row and your roll starts growing back.

How high can you go?

Climb past sink height, the bathroom mirror and the ceiling, then keep going: rooftop, cloud nine, and all the way to orbit. The view changes as you climb, from bathroom tiles to open sky, sunset, and stars.

🧪 In Development

Neon Swarm

A one-thumb roguelite arcade shooter. Drag anywhere to fly — your ship fires automatically, so all you have to think about is where to be. Kills drop XP that levels you up mid-run, letting you pick one of three upgrade cards on the fly. Chain kills for a combo multiplier, and watch out: every 5th wave is a boss.

Built different

Every sprite, sound, and effect in Neon Swarm is generated entirely in code — no imported art or audio. It's a different kind of build for Potty Games, and it shows in the look: clean neon shapes on a deep-space backdrop instead of our usual cartoon style.

Details

📱 iOS & Android (planned) ⚙️ Built in Unity 🆓 Free, optional ads only 🚀 Roguelite auto-shooter

  • Pick 1-of-3 upgrade cards on every level up

  • Permanent shop upgrades make every run stronger than the last
