Snap & Swing

A single-tap 2D physics arcade game. Your orb continuously rises — tap to fire a tether at the nearest anchor point, hold to swing around it, and release to launch yourself toward the next one. Dodge hazards, chain stars together, and see how far one run can take you.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to tether to the nearest anchor
  • Hold to keep swinging and build momentum
  • Release at the right moment to launch and dodge barriers
  • Chain stars together for combo score
