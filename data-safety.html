Snap & Swing

Snap & Swing does not require an account and does not collect personal profile information. It uses Google AdMob to show ads, which involves the data below.

Is data encrypted in transit?

Yes — data sent to our third-party services travels over standard encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Can I request my data be deleted?

Because Snap & Swing doesn't use accounts, there's no personal profile tied to you to delete. To clear your advertising identifier, reset it in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID). For any other concern, email hello@pottygames.net.

Is this data collection required to use the app?

The advertising-related data above supports the free, ad-based version of the game. We don't currently offer a way to disable ad data collection within the app itself, but you can limit ad tracking at the device level as described above.
