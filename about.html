Our Story

About Potty Games

A small studio with a simple goal: make games worth flushing time into.

Why "Potty Games"?

Because games shouldn't take themselves too seriously. Potty Games started as one developer's itch to build the kind of pick-up-and-play arcade games that were fun for their first five seconds and every session after — no fluff, no filler, no guilt trips to keep you playing.

Snap & Swing was our first release: a tight, physics-driven arcade game built from scratch in Unity. TP Tower — a one-tap toilet-paper stacking game — is right behind it, and Neon Swarm, a one-thumb roguelite shooter, is our earliest project in active development.
