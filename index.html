Meet the studio

One small team. One big obsession: fun that respects your time.

We're not chasing infinite scroll or manipulative loops. Every Potty Games title is built around one tight, satisfying core mechanic — the kind of game you open for "just one more run" and actually mean it.

🎯

Fun first

Every game starts from a single mechanic that has to feel great in the first five seconds, before anything else gets added.

🧪

Small & scrappy

We're a lean, indie operation — that means faster iteration, honest updates, and no committee-designed features.

🔒

Respect your data

No accounts, no unnecessary tracking. We keep data collection to what's needed to run ads and keep the game stable — see our Data Safety page.

Our first title

Snap & Swing

A one-tap physics arcade game about tethering, swinging, and not smashing into things.

🚧 In Testing

Tap. Tether. Swing. Repeat.

Your orb rises on its own — tap to tether to the nearest anchor, hold to swing, and let go at just the right moment to launch past barriers and chain together stars. One thumb, endless "just one more try" energy.

  • Single-tap tether-and-swing physics
  • Dodge hazards, chain stars for combo score
  • Built for 30-second sessions or 30-minute ones
  • Free to play, with light, non-intrusive ads
