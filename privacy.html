TP Tower Privacy Policy

Potty Games · Effective September 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the TP Tower mobile game ("the app") handles, published by Potty Games ("we", "us"), and the choices you have.

What we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked roll styles and settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that progress.

Advertising

TP Tower is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. When ads load, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects some information from your device to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve its services. That information includes:

  • Your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA where you allow it
  • How you interact with ads and the app, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

This data is sent to Google, is encrypted in transit, and is handled under Google's policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We don't receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off.

Your choices

  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Short ads may also play between runs, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase turns those off.
  • Where required, for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice in the app's privacy settings where available.
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can allow or deny tracking when prompted, and change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Children

TP Tower is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

We don't store your data on our own servers. To remove the progress saved on your device, uninstall the app. To learn about data held by Google for advertising, use the links above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
